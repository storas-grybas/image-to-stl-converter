Image to STL Converter

Turn a JPG, PNG or WebP into a printable heightmap relief or a flat lithophane STL, sized in millimeters. The page reads the picture with the browser's canvas, builds a closed mesh in JavaScript and saves the STL to your downloads, so nothing is uploaded. Pick a mode, set the width and thickness, check the 3D preview and download.
